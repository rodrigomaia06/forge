# Plans and routines

- Plans contain ordered routines; a routine may also exist without a plan.
- A routine starts with an empty name, a workout type, an optional centred note, optional named attributes whose values may be supplied later during a workout, and a reusable exercise/set structure. Its plan membership is not repeated as editor copy.
- Users can create, visualize, edit, move, delete, and start routines. Starting a routine creates an independent active workout, so later edits do not alter an in-progress session.
- The Plans screen groups standalone routines and plans separately. Routine rows use their workout-type icon, and swiping left on a routine or plan reveals its destructive Delete action.
- Tapping a routine, whether standalone or inside a plan, opens the shared Forge action sheet. It offers Visualize, Edit, and Start; its workout-type icon remains the direct Start control. Plan actions are Add routine and Delete plan. Swiping left remains the destructive Delete path for both routine placements and plans.
- Plans and routines are local-only and persist through the Forge repository boundary during the prototype-storage phase.
- The Plans header action presents New workout, New routine, and New workout plan from an anchored menu. The Dashboard creation action routes into Plans and opens this same menu. New workout starts immediately. New routines, including routines added to a plan, open as local editor drafts and are persisted only by an explicit Save; Cancel or Back asks to discard unsaved changes. Routine attributes are user-defined names with optional values, such as Mood or Location. The routine editor reuses the Live Workout exercise sections, including set editing, options, swipe-to-delete, and a dedicated long-press drag handle for reordering. Plan headers omit the routine count, use the standard 56 pt row height, and act as their options control; routine rows act as their action controls. Both use visible chevrons instead of overflow ellipses; the plan header is separated from its routines only by a thin inset divider.
- New standalone routines are created from that header action only; the Plans list does not expose a bottom creation button.

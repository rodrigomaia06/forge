# Live Workout

- The bottom navigation dock may compact while the Live Workout content is scrolled, but must remain visible and restore its full size when touched or when navigating to another tab. Primary tab changes use a short cross-fade plus one persistent selection indicator moving to the active item; they never use a push or lateral slide. Retapping the active tab preserves its current navigation and scroll position. Its state changes should be immediate and smooth, without an additional compact-to-expanded entrance transition. It remains visually separated from the bottom system indicator with a light lower fade. Starting a workout smoothly expands the dock to its five-item width; it contracts again when the workout ends. An active workout replaces the dock's generic play glyph with the workout type's semantic icon, without applying the type colour to dock chrome.
- The Live Workout list must reserve enough bottom space for the persistent dock so the dashed-outline `Add exercise` action remains reachable after adding many exercises.
- Elapsed and rest timers derive from timestamps and refresh together once per second; active-workout persistence is debounced during ordinary edits, while finishing or cancelling remains immediate.
- Sets and exercise cards reveal compact contextual delete actions with a left swipe; the action remains hidden in the resting state.
- The Add exercises picker dismisses its keyboard when its list is touched or dragged, without preventing the requested list action.
- If a focused weight or reps field would sit too close to the number keyboard, Live Workout and the Routine Editor temporarily scroll it into the comfortable middle of the visible area. They use native keyboard insets where available and their prior scroll position returns when the keyboard is dismissed.
- Live Workout and History workout detail reserve symmetric header space so titles remain centered. Back and Cancel are icon-only actions with accessible names; Finish and More retain visible labels.

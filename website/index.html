Get Forge

Room for how you train.

Record the session

Weight and reps, time or distance, with last session’s numbers beside every set. The rest timer follows you to the Lock Screen.

Make it repeatable

Save routines on their own or group them into plans. Starting one never changes it unless you choose to update it.

Look back, move forward

Progress charts for every exercise, a muscle heatmap and your own measurements, on a dashboard you arrange.

Bring your history

Import Hevy, Strong, FitNotes or Strive. Review uncertain exercise matches before saving.

Your training, at a glance.

From the first set to the full picture. Tap a screen for a closer look.

Your journal stays yours.

Forge works offline, without an account, cloud sync, advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. Training data stays in a local database.

Export a portable backup when you want one. Keep it outside the app so your history survives a lost device or uninstall.

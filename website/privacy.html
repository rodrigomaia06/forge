Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

I build Forge: Training Journal, an offline workout journal for iOS and Android. This policy covers the app and this website.

Data in the app

Forge stores workouts, routines, plans, custom exercises, measurements and preferences locally on your device. It does not have an account system or cloud sync, and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. I do not receive your training data through the app.

Imports, exports and diagnostics

You choose when to import a file, export a backup or share content. Exported files can contain your training history and notes. Where you save or send them is up to you and the services you select.

Local diagnostic logs record limited technical events rather than workout names, exercise names, measurements or notes. Logs are only shared when you choose to export and send them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Backup & Export → Delete all app data to clear your local Forge content. Uninstalling the app can also remove its local database. Backups saved outside the app are separate copies; delete them where you stored them.

Device services

Rest-timer notifications and iOS Live Activities use device services. Your operating system, device backups and file providers may handle data under their own settings and privacy policies.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. I have not added analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or external font services. If you choose a theme, this site remembers that preference in your browser’s local storage. You can remove it by clearing site data.

GitHub processes information such as IP addresses when serving the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement. External links open services with their own privacy policies.

Questions and updates

Contact me through the Forge issue tracker for privacy questions. Issues are public, so do not attach personal training data, private backups or credentials. I will update this page if Forge’s data handling changes.

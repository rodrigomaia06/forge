Forge support

I maintain Forge on GitHub. Use the issue tracker to report a problem or suggest a feature.

Open an issue

Reporting a problem

Include your device model, operating system, Forge version and the steps that reproduce the problem. A screenshot can help explain what happened.

GitHub issues are public. Remove personal details from screenshots and do not upload private workout backups or account credentials.

Protect your history

Before reinstalling or clearing app data, export a backup from Settings → Backup & Export and save it outside Forge.

Installing test builds

The installation section links to the iOS and Android workflows. Download artifacts from completed runs; these builds are for device testing.

Importing workouts

Use Import from another app under Backup & Export. Forge supports Hevy, Strong and FitNotes CSV files, and Strive database backups. Review the detected content and any unresolved exercise matches before saving.

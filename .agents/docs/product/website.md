# Public website

- Forge has a public product website hosted on GitHub Pages. Its repository description and homepage link should reflect Forge as a training journal for iOS and Android, without limiting its scope to strength training.
- Public author copy uses first-person singular. Preserve Forge’s established visual identity and use real product captures when presenting the app.
- The landing hero follows the accepted sculptural reference direction, using Forge’s font and brand. The phone mockup must have credible phone proportions and fit the real app capture without stretching its interface; a decorative hand may support the composition. The hero and gallery share the same iPhone hardware frame, and the video fits its display without letterboxing, while keeping the app header and dock clear of the camera cutout and lower edge. Its muted inline video shows high-resolution real Forge tab changes at a brisk pace with populated sample data in the selected theme, retains a still-image fallback, uses the phone itself as an accessible pause/play target without a separate visible video-control button, pauses off-screen or in a hidden tab, and respects reduced motion.
- The browser favicon matches the header logo’s rounded-square shape and preserves the Forge artwork. The header presents a legible Forge logo and name without the Training Journal subtitle. Screenshot browsing preserves six real app screens across both themes; useful icons supplement clear action labels. Gallery captures use high-resolution source images inside recognisable, externally sourced iPhone hardware bezels on raised panels. Capture the gallery at the frame’s display proportions with consistent top and bottom display insets. Preserve the app image proportions, with a straight centre phone and gently tilted side phones on desktop. Mobile previews remain straight and centred.
- Header navigation is grouped in a rounded pill. The theme action displays only a sun/moon icon with an accessible name and a full touch target.
- Navigation hover and press feedback uses the rounded tonal highlight of Forge’s bottom tabs. On phones, keep the hero call to action beside the hand-held mockup without clipping the hand or exposing the original phone border, centre the browsed screenshot, and keep the Source code link clearly aligned in the footer.
- Keep website publishing separate from native app build dispatches and unfinished app changes.
